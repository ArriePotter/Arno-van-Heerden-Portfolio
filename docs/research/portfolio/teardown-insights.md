# Portfolio teardown: insights

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Method:** a teardown of 4 sites (one rater: Arno), using the Teardown card in Figma (🔬 Research → 01 Portfolio teardown)
- **Sites:** rauno.me, dsgnpixl.co, sananes.co, sierrahopkins.com (weak)
- **Confidence:** directional. Four sites and one rater aren't enough to prove anything, so the interviews will test these.

| # | Pattern | Evidence (card scores) | So what for us |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The "why" behind design decisions is rare | Evidence of "why": 0/5 on three sites; Rauno 4/5 (shown with components and code) | Lead with the why, and show it rather than only tell it |
| 2 | Keyboard accessibility fails across the board | Tab focus was broken or partial on all 4 | Passing WCAG 2.2 AA is a differentiator, not just a floor |
| 3 | Strong first screens have one focal point and state the role in big type | Hierarchy 4–5 on Rauno and Sananes; dsgnpixl leads with a brand name (positioning 3/5) | Put the role first, with one focal point |
| 4 | Wayfinding inside case studies is weak | Nav & IA 2–4: no back button, case studies open in new windows, no "you are here" | Chapter navigation with an active state |
| 5 | Motion helps when it guides reading and hurts when it decorates | Rauno's scroll dot and Sananes' page transitions helped; Sierra and Sananes' hover text slowed reading | Motion only where it guides attention |
