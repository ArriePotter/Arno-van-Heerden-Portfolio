# 0004: Skip design-lead interviews in Discover; test with the v1 prototype instead

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** UX research
- **Phase:** Discover
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The brief planned 3–5 interviews with design leads in Discover to test risks #1, #3 and #6 (`docs/projects/portfolio/brief.md` §9). Arno has no design contacts, and recruiting strangers would likely take months. The v1 time box ends on 28 Nov 2026 (decision 0003).

## Options considered

1. **Recruit strangers on LinkedIn now.** This is the real test, but the slow lead time would block the phase.
2. **Book free mentor sessions with senior designers (e.g. ADPList).** Faster, but it still costs time now, when there's nothing to show yet.
3. **Skip interviews in Discover; get feedback from senior designers on the v1 prototype instead.** No blocking, and the feedback lands on a real artefact rather than hypotheticals.

## Decision

Option 3. Discover continues with the teardown, moodboard and desk research. Risks #1, #3 and #6 remain open until the prototype is reviewed.

## Evidence

*Judgement.* The teardown (`docs/research/portfolio/teardown-insights.md`) is one rater looking at four sites, so it's directional only.

## Consequences

- **Gate skipped and logged (lifecycle rule):** Define will make positioning choices (risk #3) and venture-framing choices (risk #6) without outside validation.
- **Mitigation:** before Handoff, get at least 2 senior designers to review the v1 prototype, for example through free mentor sessions. Revisit any choice they reject.

## Case-study note

With no network in design, I moved validation to the point where I had something real to show, and recorded the risk instead of hiding it.
