# 0005: Project Khoisān is Case Study 1; Finanza is dropped

- **Date:** 2026-10-18 (decided 5 Oct 2026, confirmed 17 Oct 2026)
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** Product strategy
- **Phase:** Discover
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The roadmap planned Finanza, a redesign exercise, as Case Study 1. Meanwhile Arno designed and shipped Project Khoisān: a real training app for the MUT 60 trail race, with real users (Arno and Daan), its own repo, decision log, research and Figma file.

## Options considered

1. **Keep Finanza as Case Study 1.** It follows the original plan, but it's a practice redesign with no real users or outcomes.
2. **Make Khoisān Case Study 1 and keep Finanza for later.** This splits time across two case studies before v1 exists.
3. **Make Khoisān Case Study 1 and drop Finanza entirely.**

## Decision

Option 3. Khoisān is Case Study 1, and every Finanza item in the roadmap is dropped.

## Evidence

- NN/g's survey of 204 hiring managers found they want to see real process and the value produced, not just final screens (`docs/research/portfolio/desk/evidence-ledger.md`). Khoisān has a recorded process, and Finanza doesn't.
- *Judgement:* a shipped product with real users beats a hypothetical redesign.

## Consequences

- The case-study story comes from Khoisān's own `docs/` and Figma. The portfolio only tells the story; it doesn't duplicate those records (CLAUDE.md #9).
- Khoisān skipped some gates (logged in its own decisions), and the case study must say so honestly.
- The roadmap's Stage 1–4 Finanza work is freed up for visual fundamentals.

## Case-study note

I swapped a practice redesign for a product I shipped to real users, because real users make for a real case study.
