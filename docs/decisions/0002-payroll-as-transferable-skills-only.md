# 0002: Payroll appears only as transferable skills, never as identity

- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Status:** Accepted (refines the original "never mention payroll" rule)
- **Department:** Content design
- **Phase:** Brief
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The original rule banned any mention of payroll, to stop Arno being pigeonholed. In practice that hides real, relevant experience: working in teams, under pace and pressure, on complex multi-country workflows at a B2B SaaS company. The actual risk is narrower. Reviewers must not conclude that Arno is "a payroll person who designs as a hobby".

## Options considered

1. **Keep the total ban.**
   - Pros: no pigeonholing risk.
   - Cons: loses legitimate evidence of teamwork and pace, and creates an awkward gap in the story.
2. **Mention payroll freely.**
   - Cons: invites the exact misreading we want to avoid.
3. **Mention the experience only as transferable skills.**
   - Pros: keeps the evidence and keeps the identity clear.

## Decision

Option 3. Deel experience may appear **only as evidence of transferable skills**, such as teamwork, pace, stakes, complex workflows and B2B SaaS. Payroll is never the headline, the identity or a claimed expertise. Design is presented as the profession Arno is pursuing, not a side interest.

## Evidence

Judgement (Arno's call), consistent with decision 0001 (lead as Product Designer).

## Consequences

- `CLAUDE.md` non-negotiable #5 has been updated to match.
- Content design checks every mention: does it show a skill, or does it suggest an identity?
