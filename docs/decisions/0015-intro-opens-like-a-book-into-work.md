# 0015: Intro opens like a book into Work, and the case-study cards bend around the title

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** Motion design / Interaction design
- **Phase:** Build (reverse mode, 0011)
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The site is "the book" (Direction A). The step from the Intro page into Work is the moment the book opens, and Work holds only two case studies for now.

## Options considered

1. **Zoom into the middle ring** between the statements, revealing Work inside it. Built first; rejected: "the scroll should not increase the text size".
2. **The book opens:** the statement rows fade, the last row splits apart, Work appears in the widening gap. Arno's reference sketch.
3. For the cards: **rising cards that part and rest beside the title** (built), then **a circle cut out of full-width cards** (built; read as "going under a white circle"), then **cards that bend around an invisible circle** (built, kept).

## Decision

- **Book opening (scrubbed to the scroll):** the top statement row fades, then the second row; then "Curious" slides off left and "about why" off right, and Work opens in the gap (clip-path). The background Why is wiped away by the opening. The statement grid is two equal columns, so the split is on the exact centre line.
- **Work cards:** two half-width cards (page margin to a 14 px gap), 66% of the screen tall, rise from the bottom and **bend around an invisible circle** round the "Work" title: each inner edge is redrawn per frame as a bell curve, and each line of text shifts sideways with the bend. They rest with the title in the bend. No borders (tried twice, removed). The Khoisān card is a link to its case study.
- **Rejected along the way:** ring zoom, mask cut-out, a blurred edge, a sand-hourglass experiment, plain and hand-drawn borders.

## Evidence

- Arno's sketches and reference screenshots (19 Oct). Moodboard: "scrolling *into* the portfolio… a spacious 3D effect".
- *Judgement:* the cut-out read as a hole, not a bend; deforming the edges and shifting the text makes the circle read as solid.

## Consequences

- The Intro → Work stretch is a sticky scene (150 svh opening + 100 svh cards), so the nav's Work position is at the end of the opening.
- Reduced motion: the pages cross-fade and the cards sit in their bent resting shape.

## Case-study note

The home page reads like opening a book: the last line of the intro splits, and the work is what's inside.
