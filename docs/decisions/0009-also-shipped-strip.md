# 0009: Show other shipped products as a small "Also shipped" strip

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** Accepted
- **Department:** Product strategy / Content design
- **Phase:** Explore
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

Arno has shipped products that have no recorded process: KhayaPay (iOS), an agentic bookkeeping web app (Axiomatics, Arno's own company), and an MTN data visualisation tool. The question was whether to show them, and how, next to the two full case studies (decision 0007). Brief risk #6 covers how to show Arno's own ventures.

## Options considered

1. **Don't show them.** Safest, but it hides evidence that Arno ships products end to end (decision 0006).
2. **Show them as full case-study cards.** They have no process to show, so they can't "prove the why", and they would dilute the two real case studies.
3. **A small "Also shipped" strip after the case studies:** one image, one line and a link each.

## Decision

Option 3, with **KhayaPay** and the **agentic bookkeeping web app**. The **MTN tool is excluded**: it was contract work, and there's no permission to show it.

Framing rules:

- **Bookkeeping app:** presented as a product Arno designed and shipped, never as a company Arno runs (brief risk #6).
- **KhayaPay:** an iOS app and what it does for its users, with no payroll angle (decision 0002).

## Evidence

- NN/g survey of 204 hiring managers: quality over quantity (`docs/research/portfolio/desk/evidence-ledger.md`).
- Decision 0006: "I research, design and ship products end to end" needs visible proof of shipping.
- *Judgement:* contract work is never shown without the client's permission.

## Consequences

- These products don't count towards the "5+ pieces → brick wall" trigger in 0007, unless one gets a full case study.
- Risk #6 is only partly resolved; test the framing in the prototype review (decision 0004).

## Case-study note

I showed range without diluting depth: two full case studies, and a short strip of other products I've shipped.
