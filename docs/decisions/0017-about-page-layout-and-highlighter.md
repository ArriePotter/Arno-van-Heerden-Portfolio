# 0017: About page: statement, photo flush left, a glass text card and a hand-drawn highlighter

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** Accepted (copy and photo are placeholders)
- **Department:** Visual design / Content design / Motion design
- **Phase:** Build (reverse mode, 0011)
- **Decided by:** Arno

## Context

The About page follows a reference Arno chose (a dark portfolio with a statement headline, a photo bleeding off the left edge, and text columns). It also starts the skills and tools, which are the beginning of the next page.

## Decision

- **Layout:** a statement headline (muted, with the key words in full colour); a photo frame flush with the left screen edge (right corners rounded); a frosted-glass card (backdrop blur inside a thin border, top aligned with the photo) with the opening quote and two stacked paragraphs.
- **Highlighter:** key phrases get a hand-drawn marker stroke (wavy edges, slight tilt per phrase) that draws in reading order as you scroll: one phrase at a time, left to right, never two at once, smoothly eased and allowed to trail the scroll. The words keep their colour.
- **Skills strip:** an endless strip of skills and tools at the top of the Get in touch page, which slides over the stuck photo (0016). Pauses on hover; still with reduced motion.

## Evidence

- Arno's reference screenshots (19 Oct).
- Content: the key phrases are the claims a hiring manager should see on a skim (NN/g: people scan; evidence ledger).
- *Judgement:* a colour change read as a link state; a highlighter reads as emphasis.

## Consequences

- All copy is placeholder text drawn from decisions 0005, 0006 and 0009, for Arno to rewrite. No payroll angle (0002).
- The skills list and logos are placeholders until Arno sends his list.

## Case-study note

On About, the scroll highlights what matters, one phrase at a time, so a skim still lands the key points.
